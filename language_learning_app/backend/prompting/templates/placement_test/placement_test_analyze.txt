═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCORING METHODOLOGY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For OBJECTIVE sections (Reading, Listening, Vocab/Grammar, Translation):
• Calculate % correct at each CEFR level (A0, A1, A2, B1, B2, C1).
• A learner passes a level when they score >= 60% on questions targeting that level.
• The highest level where they pass >= 60% forms the objective baseline.
• If a learner scores 0% or near 0% on A1 questions, assign A0.

For SUBJECTIVE sections (Writing, Speaking):
• Holistically grade each response on the CEFR descriptors:
  - A0: Cannot produce meaningful output; single isolated words or nothing at all.
  - A1: Very basic phrases, formulaic language, minimal vocabulary.
  - A2: Simple sentences, familiar topics, limited range.
  - B1: Straightforward connected text, copes with unfamiliar situations.
  - B2: Clear detailed text on various subjects, discusses pros and cons.
  - C1: Well-structured, fluent, complex ideas with nuance.
• Score each response as an estimated CEFR level (A0-C1).

Skill weights for final level:
  Reading:            15%
  Listening:          15%
  Vocabulary/Grammar: 15%
  Translation:        10%
  Transliteration:    10%
  Writing:            15%
  Speaking:           10%
  Conversation:       10%

Final level = weighted average (map A0=0, A1=1, A2=2, B1=3, B2=4, C1=5, then round to nearest level).

If a learner left a subjective section entirely blank, weight that section as 0 (redistribute to objective sections proportionally).

CRITICAL JSON OUTPUT RULES

7. In "item_feedback", include ONLY items the learner got WRONG (incorrect MCQ choices or blank subjective responses). For each wrong item_id, give one concise explanation of the correct answer or what the learner missed.
